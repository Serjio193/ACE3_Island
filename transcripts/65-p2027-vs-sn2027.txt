# Video 65 — P2027 vs SN2027 replacement behavior

Cleaned technical transcript. Greetings, soldering dead time, phone-call interruption, music, camera/UI chatter, repetition, and shopping commentary removed.

## 0:30–1:42 — Test setup

The author describes two A3 variants seen in donor boards:
- SN-family controller,
- P2027/P-family controller.

In his donor sample, P variants appeared frequently and several came from iPhone 15 Plus boards.

Experiment: install a **P-family A3** into the test iPhone 15 Plus board in place of the prior SN-family setup.

## 6:25–8:16 — Initial behavior

With the donor P A3 installed but the recipient external ROM unchanged:
- host detects PortDFU,
- original-screen touch does not work,
- USB subsystem is not functioning normally.

There is no simple model mismatch: the donor A3 is from a 15 Plus and the recipient is also a 15 Plus.

The pre-boot model/identity information is coming from the A3 path rather than the AP because normal USB bridging is not active.

## 10:09–11:37 — Apple/Finder refuses A3 authorization

Finder restore fails.

The restore log again reaches checkpoint **131D / Update A3** and includes the TSS refusal:

**“Declined to authorize this image on this device for this user.”**

Important result: matching the broad phone model is not sufficient when the A3 variant/revision does not match the expected configuration.

## 12:54–14:43 — 3uTools also fails while only PortDFU is available

Unlike the prior cross-model SN experiment, the device does not successfully transition into normal recovery after the Finder failure; it remains in PortDFU.

3uTools also fails early.

The log reaches:

**“received USB port controller one ticket, creating A3 binary”**

but the process does not complete successfully.

## 15:09–23:40 — Transfer the ROM paired with the donor P A3

The author programs the external ROM with the backup corresponding to that donor P A3.

After installing the matching ROM:
- A3 boots,
- the device is no longer stuck in PortDFU,
- Finder sees normal recovery mode.

This shows that in this P-vs-SN scenario, relying on automatic ROM recovery was not enough; the matching donor ROM was required to get the A3 to boot persistently.

## 24:06–26:35 — 3uTools restore now succeeds

With the matching P-A3 ROM installed, 3uTools restore succeeds.

Afterward:
- touch works,
- charging works,
- USB communication works.

## 26:47–27:49 — Practical conclusion

The phone appears functional under 3uTools, but Finder/iTunes is still expected to refuse future standard Apple restore/update because the A3 type does not match the original configuration.

The author's repair recommendation:
- replace **SN with SN**,
- replace **P with P**.

For this experiment, unlike the earlier same-revision SN swap, **matching external ROM transfer was required** before the donor P A3 could operate normally.

## Research significance

This experiment separates three constraints:
1. phone model,
2. A3 silicon variant/revision,
3. external ROM/application image.

Same phone model alone does not guarantee Apple A3 personalization/restore acceptance.
